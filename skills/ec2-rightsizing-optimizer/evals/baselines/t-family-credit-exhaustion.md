# Baseline (no-skill) response: t-family-credit-exhaustion

---

Your t3.large is running out of CPU credits during business hours,
which is causing the latency spikes. The simplest fix is to enable
Unlimited mode, which lets the instance spend additional credits as
needed (you pay for the overage).

Alternatively, you could upgrade to a larger t-family instance (like
t3.xlarge) or migrate to an m-family instance that doesn't have the
credit system.
